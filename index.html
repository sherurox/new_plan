System design is the one real gap

Zero background, and it's binary — you either have a delivery framework and the vocabulary, or you visibly don't. Six of the seventeen steps are here for a reason.

Cold re-solving beats new problems

Reading your own past solution and feeling recognition is not retrieval practice, and the evidence it transfers is weak. Re-solve from scratch with the old code closed.

Calibration is your differentiator

You proved an exact law for how temperature scaling distorts a Bayes-error estimator. Almost nobody discusses calibration past "we use temperature scaling." Learn to steer evaluation questions there.
