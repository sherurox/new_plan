System design is the one real gap

Zero background, and it's binary — you either have the vocabulary and a delivery framework, or you visibly don't. Three blocks a week closes it in six weeks. This is the module to open first.

Cold re-solving, not new problems

Reading your own past solution and feeling recognition is not retrieval practice, and the evidence that it transfers is weak. Re-solve from scratch a week later with the old code closed. If you can't reproduce it, it goes back in the queue.

Calibration is your differentiator

You proved an exact law for how temperature scaling distorts a Bayes-error estimator. Almost no candidate discusses calibration past "we use temperature scaling." Learn to steer evaluation-metric questions there.
